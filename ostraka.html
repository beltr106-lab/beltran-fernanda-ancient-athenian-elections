Ostraka Script

Ostraka

Ostraka were shards of pottery used as a special type of voting ballot used for ostracism and exile. If a public figure in Athens was too disliked or too popular, they could be ostracized or exiled for 10 years. In this type of election, members of the Assembly would scratch a name into a shard of pottery of a person who they thought deserved to be exiled.

Greek Majuscule

The script often seen in ostraka are in fact Greek Majuscules. This style of writing was developed from the carving of letters into stone, and was used from the 4th century BCE to the 9th century BCE. This script was written in bilinear formatting or capital letters, and without spaces between words.
